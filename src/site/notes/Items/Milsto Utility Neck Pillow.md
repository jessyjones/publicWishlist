## Milsto Utility Neck Pillow

![|640](https://cdn.japantrendshop.com/img/thumbs/10113/bruno-milsto-utility-neck-pillow-thumb.jpg)

The **Milsto Utility Neck Pillow** looks attractive even before you realize that it's much more than just a neck pillow, and that actually offers several other solutions to small but persistent everyday problems!

The key here is versatility and Bruno's Milsto Utility Neck Pillow offers it in spades: roll it up and you have a 580 x 190 x 100 mm (22.8 x 7.5 x 3.9") neck pillow with an adjustable strap to bring it exactly to the length you want it. Keep one side up and it also becomes a 480 x 250 mm (18.9 x 9.8") hood for separating you from the rest of the world when you nap or rest. Open it out completely and get a 1,160 x 600 mm (3.8 x 2 ft) item that you can use as a shawl or a throw to cover the lower part of your body.
